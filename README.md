Questions and Answers

1. Why is timely delivery crucial in software project management, and how can project managers ensure deadlines are met?

Timely delivery helps maintain client trust, reduces costs, and prevents scope creep. Project managers can meet deadlines by setting realistic timelines, incorporating buffer periods, regularly tracking progress, and ensuring clear communication across the team.


2. How does effective cost control contribute to the success of a software project? What strategies can be used to prevent budget overruns?

Cost control ensures project profitability and resource efficiency. Strategies include accurate budgeting, regular cost monitoring, using contingency reserves, avoiding scope creep, and conducting periodic financial reviews.


3. Compare and contrast Agile and Waterfall methodologies. What are the main advantages and disadvantages of each?

*Agile:* 
- *Advantages:* Flexible, iterative, promotes customer feedback  
- *Disadvantages:* Can lack structure, harder to predict timelines

**Waterfall:**  
- *Advantages:* Well-documented, easy to manage in fixed-scope projects  
- *Disadvantages:* Inflexible to change, delayed feedback  



4. In what types of projects might Agile be more beneficial than Waterfall, and vice versa? Can you provide examples of each?

*Agile* suits projects with evolving requirements like mobile app development.  
*Waterfall* works best in fixed-scope projects like government or construction software.


5. What are some methods for ensuring quality assurance throughout a software project? Why is it important to maintain high standards?

Methods include automated testing, peer reviews, CI/CD, and user acceptance testing. High quality prevents bugs, saves costs, and builds user trust.


6. How does defining the project scope contribute to successful project planning? What is a Work Breakdown Structure (WBS), and why is it useful?

Defining scope avoids misunderstandings and scope creep. A WBS breaks down the project into manageable parts, making it easier to estimate time and assign responsibilities.


7. What are the benefits of developing a detailed project schedule, and how can Gantt charts assist in this process?

A detailed schedule helps in resource planning, milestone tracking, and risk management. Gantt charts visualize task sequences, deadlines, and dependencies for better coordination.
